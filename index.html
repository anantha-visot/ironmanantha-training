Your millet toolkit

Ragi (finger millet)

Your recovery hero — richest in calcium & iron. Ragi malt/porridge for breakfast and post-workout shakes; ragi dosa/roti.

Bajra (pearl millet)

Iron & magnesium for endurance. Bajra roti and khichdi; great in cooler months and around heavy sessions.

Jowar (sorghum)

Light, versatile staple carb. Jowar roti, upma, or as a rice swap at lunch.

Foxtail millet

Lower-GI, steady energy — ideal pre-long-ride. Foxtail pulao/upma or a grain bowl base.

Little / kodo millet

Easy rice substitute. Use in khichdi with moong dal for a complete-protein dinner.

Pair for protein

Millets alone are moderate protein — always pair with dal/rajma, paneer/curd, tofu or a whey/plant scoop to complete the amino profile.

Train the gut on the bike — build from 60 toward 90 g carbs/hr over the weeks. Post-session: ~1–1.2 g/kg carbs (a millet bowl or ragi malt) + 20–30 g protein within the hour. Do a sweat test (weigh nude before/after a 1-h session) to set your exact sodium.

Micronutrients & supplements
Get baseline bloods early (ferritin/iron, B12, vitamin D) — low iron silently wrecks endurance. Ragi and bajra are iron- and calcium-rich, and pairing iron meals with vitamin C (lemon, citrus) while keeping tea/coffee away from them boosts absorption. Then discuss with a doctor or sports dietitian: B12 (sensible to supplement without eggs), vitamin D (if low), omega-3 DHA/EPA (algae-based, or daily flax/chia/walnuts), iron (only if labs are low), and optionally creatine 3–5 g/day (vegetarians respond well; supports strength & recovery). General guidance, not medical advice.
